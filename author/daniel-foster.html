About the author

Daniel Foster is a legal writer and consumer advocacy specialist with a deep focus on civil litigation, tort law, and workplace rights. Over his career, Daniel has broken down complex legal statutes, workers' compensation frameworks, and defendant rights into transparent, actionable resources. His work helps everyday readers understand their legal rights and options following motor vehicle accidents, workplace disputes, and consumer injury claims.

Our articles are for general information and are not personalized legal, financial, or professional advice.
