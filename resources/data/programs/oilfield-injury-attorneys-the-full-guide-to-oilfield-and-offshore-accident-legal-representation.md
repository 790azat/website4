## Offshore Injury Claims

An **offshore injury lawyer** may handle cases involving workers injured on offshore platforms, vessels, rigs, and other maritime environments.

Depending on the circumstances, offshore claims may involve federal or maritime laws in addition to other workplace injury rules. The worker's occupation, location of the accident, employer relationship, and involvement of a vessel can affect which legal framework applies.
